Common Questions

How do I unlock everything?

MoodRx is free to use. A one-time Own It purchase unlocks all workouts, your full history, the supplement tracker, and the AI coach. MoodRx+ is an optional subscription that adds coach voices and more, and starts with a 7-day free trial.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Manage or cancel anytime in your store account: on iPhone, Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Free trials convert to paid unless cancelled before the trial ends.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you reinstall or switch devices, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Your history is stored on your device. You can clear it anytime from Settings in the app, or by deleting the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is the voice venting private?

Your speech is turned into text on your device when possible; if it can't be, your platform (Apple or Google) transcribes it. The text is sent to our AI provider to write a reply — nothing is stored or used to train AI. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

MoodRx is a wellness app, not a medical or crisis service. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S. Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room.
